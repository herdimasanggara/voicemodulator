how to use it
  1. Open this page over https (host it on your site, or on any static host). Browsers refuse the microphone on plain http or on a file opened from the phone's storage.
  2. Tap Start, allow the mic. Your voice comes out of the device's speaker, changed, and glides to a new voice on the interval.
  3. Phone rig: point the phone's speaker at the laptop mic and keep the phone's own mic (bottom edge) turned away from its speaker; lower output gain if it starts to howl. The gate mutes the output when you're not talking, which breaks the feedback loop between words.
  4. Laptop rig (cleaner): run this on the Mac with headphones on, pick BlackHole as the output, and set the call app's mic to BlackHole.
